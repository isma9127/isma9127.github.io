🤔 What is laya.cpp?

laya.cpp is a smart decision-making tool that runs directly on your computer. It's called an "inference engine" — which simply means it takes AI models that have been trained to make "typed decisions" (like classifying data, recognizing patterns, or making predictions) and runs them locally on your machine. That means your data stays private on your device, and you don't need an internet connection to use it.

Whether you're a researcher, a business professional, a student, or just curious about AI, laya.cpp makes it possible to use sophisticated AI tools without being a programmer. You just download, install, and run.

🛠️ System Requirements

Minimum Requirements (Everything):

  • Operating System: Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, or any modern Linux distribution (Ubuntu 20.04+, Fedora 35+, etc.)
  • Processor: Any 64-bit CPU (Intel Core i3, AMD Ryzen 3, Apple M1 or newer)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
  • Storage: 500 MB free disk space
  • Graphics (Optional): Any graphics card works, but for best performance:
    • NVIDIA GPU with CUDA support (GTX 900 series or newer)

If you don't have a compatible GPU, no worries! laya.cpp automatically falls back to CPU mode and still runs very efficiently.
